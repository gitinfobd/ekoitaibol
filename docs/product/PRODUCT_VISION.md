# Product Vision & Institutional Philosophy
**Working Brand:** EKOTAI BOL (একতাই বল)  
**Codename:** JANOSHOKTI  
**Tagline:** একতাই শক্তি, উদ্যোগে পরিবর্তন। (Unity is strength, innovation brings transformation)

---

## 1. The Core Problem
Across Bangladesh's 64 districts, grassroots innovators, farmers, teachers, and youths face recurring regional challenges—from cold storage deficits in Rangpur's potato belt to saline water crises in the southern coastal delta. 

Traditionally, citizens have had only two avenues:
1. Wait for centralized government budget allocations.
2. Rely on donor-driven foreign NGOs that lack regional longevity.

There has been no transparent, grassroots platform where a citizen can submit a problem, rally public evaluation, aggregate modest 100 Taka community contributions, and execute solutions locally while providing direct jobs with pension safety nets.

---

## 2. The Vision: Decentralized Social Development
EKOTAI BOL establishes an incorruptible digital civic commons:
- **Grassroots Primacy:** Innovation begins in the union and upazila, evaluated by the people who live there.
- **Voluntary & Micro:** No burden on the poor; a single 100 Taka contribution aggregated among thousands creates massive, debt-free capital.
- **Radical Transparency:** Every Taka received and spent is published on a live, immutable ledger.
- **Dignified Labor:** Project workers are not temporary day-laborers; they receive legal contracts complying with Bangladesh Labor Act 2006, along with contributions to the Pragati Universal Pension Scheme.

---

## 3. Geographical Rollout Strategy
1. **Phase 1 (Pilot):** Rangpur District (Agriculture, Solar Cold Storage, Localized Education).
2. **Phase 2 (Divisional):** Rangpur & Rajshahi Divisions (Northern Agri-Hub).
3. **Phase 3 (National):** All 8 Divisions, 64 Districts, 495 Upazilas.
