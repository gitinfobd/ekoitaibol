# Target User Personas
**Platform:** EKOTAI BOL (একতাই বল)  

---

## 1. Persona 1: Rafiqul Islam — The Grassroots Innovator
- **Age:** 34
- **Location:** Mithapukur, Rangpur
- **Occupation:** High school physics teacher & smallholder farmer
- **Goal:** Wants to build a solar-powered mini cold-storage system so local farmers don't have to sell potatoes at distress prices.
- **Pain Points:** Lack of capital, fear of corrupt middlemen stealing the idea, unable to travel to Dhaka to pitch to venture funds.
- **Platform Value:** Submits his idea for free via phone; receives AI viability scoring; remains anonymous to the public; sees his idea selected for local execution.

---

## 2. Persona 2: Tanvir Ahmed — The Empathic Micro-Donor
- **Age:** 28
- **Location:** Dhaka (originally from Gaibandha)
- **Occupation:** Junior Software Engineer
- **Goal:** Wants to give back to northern districts without money being swallowed by corrupt charity administrators.
- **Pain Points:** Distrusts traditional NGO overheads; wants proof of where every Taka goes.
- **Platform Value:** Donates 100 Taka via bKash; sees his masked phone and transaction instantly on the live public ledger; follows progress updates.

---

## 3. Persona 3: Anisur Rahman — The Platform Moderator & Governance Lead
- **Age:** 45
- **Location:** Rangpur City
- **Occupation:** Social enterprise manager & retired cooperative auditor
- **Goal:** Wants to maintain quality, filter out duplicate or fraudulent ideas, and safeguard community funds.
- **Platform Value:** Accesses `/admin` with Super Admin privileges; reviews AI analysis reports; approves viable ideas to the public notice board; creates new projects.
