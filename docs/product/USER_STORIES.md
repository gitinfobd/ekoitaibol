# User Stories Catalog
**Platform:** EKOTAI BOL (একতাই বল)  

---

## 1. Idea Submission & Screening Stories
- **US-01:** As a citizen, I want to submit a local problem and proposed solution for free using my Google account, so that I can bring attention to my community's needs without financial barrier.
- **US-02:** As an idea submitter, I want my identity and phone number kept completely confidential from the public notice board, so that I am protected from local intimidation or political harassment.
- **US-03:** As a submitter, I want AI assistance to check if my idea has already been proposed in my district, so that I don't duplicate existing initiatives.

---

## 2. Public Notice Board & Community Evaluation Stories
- **US-04:** As an anonymous visitor, I want to browse approved ideas by district and category on the notice board, so that I can discover grassroots innovations in my region.
- **US-05:** As a citizen, I want to rate approved ideas from 1 to 5 stars and submit structured feedback (Positive / Negative / Improvement), so that high-potential ideas bubble up to implementation.

---

## 3. Voluntary Funding & Transparency Stories
- **US-06:** As a donor, I want to contribute a modest 100 Taka to a specific verified project, so that I can directly support development in my home district.
- **US-07:** As a donor, I want to see my transaction immediately on the public ledger with my phone number masked, so that I have absolute proof my money was recorded without exposing my personal number.

---

## 4. Moderation & Administration Stories
- **US-08:** As a platform administrator, I want to inspect submitted ideas in a private queue and approve or reject them, so that low-quality or abusive content never reaches the public board.
- **US-09:** As an administrator, I want to create new verified projects with defined funding goals, so that community fundraising can launch for vetted ideas.
