# Business & Economic Model
**Platform:** EKOTAI BOL (একতাই বল)  

---

## 1. Non-Profit Social Enterprise Framework
EKOTAI BOL operates under a non-dividend, self-sustaining social enterprise charter. It is not an equity crowdfunding vehicle; backers do not receive financial dividends or ownership shares.

---

## 2. Revenue & Funding Streams

### 2.1 Micro-Crowdfunding (Primary)
- Voluntary community donations starting at 100 BDT.
- 100% of earmarked project donations are allocated directly to execution escrow.
- Up to 5% operational allocation may be utilized for platform infrastructure, hosting, payment gateway fees, and field inspection.

### 2.2 Institutional Grants & CSR Partnerships
- Vetted corporate social responsibility (CSR) programs matching community contributions 1:1 for critical initiatives (e.g., healthcare clinics, solar refrigeration).

---

## 3. Financial Integrity & Escrow Protocol
- **Double-Entry Accounting:** Every inflow matches an outflow or an escrow balance.
- **Milestone-Based Fund Releases:** Project capital is never disbursed in a lump sum. Funds release in tranches contingent upon verified photo and geo-tagged inspection reports.
- **Transparent Field Ledgers:** Invoices, purchase receipts, and payroll stubs are digitized and attached to project dashboards.
