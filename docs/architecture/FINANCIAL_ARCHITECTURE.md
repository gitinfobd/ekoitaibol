# Financial Architecture & Transparency Protocol
**Platform:** EKOTAI BOL (একতাই বল)  

---

## 1. The 100 Taka Civic Micro-Funding Model
Traditional non-profit models rely heavily on foreign grants or large benefactors, leading to dependency and misaligned incentives. EKOTAI BOL proves that when thousands of citizens pool 100 Taka each, large-scale district projects can be fully funded without external debt.

---

## 2. Inviolable Financial Principles
1. **Never Reset Financial Ledgers:** Under no circumstances may historical donation data or project ledgers be purged or cleared.
2. **Double Verification:** Donations require mobile wallet transaction IDs and are recorded with immutable timestamps.
3. **Privacy Preserving Transparency:** Donor phone numbers are masked (`0171****890`) to protect individual privacy while maintaining public auditability.
4. **Milestone Tranche Escrow:** Project execution funds are released in phases (e.g. 30% Foundation, 40% Procurement, 30% Commissioning) subject to local verification.
