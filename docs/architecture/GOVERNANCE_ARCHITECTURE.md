# Governance, Legal & Worker Welfare Architecture
**Platform:** EKOTAI BOL (একতাই বল)  

---

## 1. Statutory Compliance: Bangladesh Labor Act 2006 (Amended 2023)
Unlike informal NGO temporary jobs, any worker employed on an EKOTAI BOL project is provided a formalized employment framework:
- **Section 28 Compliance:** Transparent appointment letter specifying job scope, standard 48-hour weekly work schedule, overtime rates, and safety gear.
- **Equal Pay:** Zero gender wage disparity across equivalent tasks.
- **Provident Fund Participation:** Project operational budgets reserve 8.33% employer matching contributions for employee welfare funds.

---

## 2. Universal Pension Scheme (সার্বজনীন পেনশন স্কিম — প্রগতি)
Under the National Pension Authority (NPA) Act, all project team members and full-time coordinators are enrolled in the **Pragati (প্রগতি)** scheme:
- Monthly contributions shared between project operational budgets and employee payroll.
- Provides guaranteed monthly pension starting from age 60, ensuring long-term dignity and security for grassroots organizers.

---

## 3. Anti-Fraud & Ethics Code
- Board members and Super Admins cannot draw private profits or dividends from community funding campaigns.
- Family members of project coordinators cannot be awarded single-source execution contracts.
- All procurement over 50,000 BDT requires three competitive bids published on the project dashboard.
